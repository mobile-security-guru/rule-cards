# 2027 rule calendar

Dates that the monthly Rule Cards cover. Where sources differ on the exact day, the entry says so.

- **End of 2026:** Every EU member state must offer a national eID wallet (the exact December day varies by source).
- **Jul 10, 2027:** Key obligations of the EU Anti-Money Laundering Regulation apply.
- **2027, mid to year end:** Relying parties requiring strong authentication must accept the wallets (sources split between mid-2027 and end of 2027, so no day is printed).
- **Dec 11, 2027:** The main Cyber Resilience Act obligations apply. Reporting duties have applied since Sep 11, 2026.
- **Ongoing:** 22 of 27 member states had a NIS2 law in force by Jul 18, 2026.

Taught session: second Tuesday of each month, 7pm ET.
